# NoxaPlus PDF Desk Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The NoxaPlus PDF Desk community aims to make participation welcoming, respectful, and constructive, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality, appearance, race, religion, sexual orientation, experience, or technical background.

Everyone participating in project spaces, including maintainers, is expected to follow this code.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and professional. Focus feedback on the work, not the person.
- Listen to different perspectives, offer constructive criticism, and accept corrections in good faith.
- Give newcomers room to learn and explain technical context without condescension.
- Respect people's time, boundaries, privacy, and choices about personal disclosure.
- Protect document privacy. Use synthetic PDFs and sanitized screenshots rather than sharing another person's files, identifying file names, or confidential information.
- Use the exact NoxaPlus brand and avoid impersonating maintainers or making unsupported claims on the project's behalf.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discriminatory language, slurs, threats, intimidation, or sustained personal attacks.
- Sexualized content, unwanted sexual attention, or inappropriate contact.
- Publishing private information or documents without permission, including contact details and identifying PDF contents.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, repeated bad-faith arguments, or targeting someone across project channels.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern, assists an investigation, or sets a boundary.
- Evading moderation decisions or misrepresenting yourself as an official project representative.

Respectful disagreement and good-faith technical criticism are welcome. They do not excuse abusive behavior.

## Scope

This code applies to official project issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other community spaces. It also applies when someone represents NoxaPlus PDF Desk publicly, such as through an official account or at an event. Relevant harassment outside these spaces may be considered when it affects community safety.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to **NoxaPlus2025@gmail.com**, using the subject `NoxaPlus PDF Desk conduct report`. Include the relevant links or dates, a factual description, and any immediate safety concerns. Send only the information needed; avoid distributing private documents as evidence.

For software vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead. Do not post a private conduct report in a public issue. If there is an immediate threat to personal safety, contact appropriate local emergency or support services.

Reports will be reviewed as privately as practical. Information may need to be shared with those responsible for handling the report; absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed. No fixed response deadline is promised.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove or edit inappropriate contributions, limit interaction, or temporarily or permanently exclude participants. Decisions should consider impact, severity, repetition, and community safety, rather than a rigid requirement to issue warnings first.

Depending on the circumstances, action may include:

1. A private request to stop or correct behavior.
2. A warning and clear participation conditions.
3. A temporary restriction from project spaces.
4. Permanent exclusion for severe or repeated misconduct.

People affected by moderation may request reconsideration through the same private contact, providing relevant context without targeting other participants. A maintainer involved in a complaint should avoid deciding it alone when another uninvolved maintainer is available.
